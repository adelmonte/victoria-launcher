Victoria Launcher is a minimal, list-based Android home screen, and an open
source alternative to Niagara Launcher.

The home screen keeps a short list of favourites under a clock and weather
widget. Everything else lives in an alphabetical app list you reach by swiping
the edge of the screen, with an A-Z scrubber for jumping straight to a letter.

Layout, icon pack, icon and text size, spacing, font and colours are all
adjustable, and an optional Now Playing widget shows current media.

No ads, no tracking, no network access.
