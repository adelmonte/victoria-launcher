New:
* Favorites can show your most-used apps instead of a list you keep.
* Separate icon switches for favorites and the app list.
* Notification counts on icons, off by default.
* Letter headings in the app list can be turned off.
* Set where a picked letter lands, for tall phones.
* Folders can close themselves after you open something in them.
* Enter in the search box launches the first result.
* HOME returns the home screen to the top and the first widget.

Fixed:
* Tapping a switch in settings could toggle it and toggle it straight back.
* Edit layout was unreadable over wallpapers that are light on one side.
* The app list shifted sideways when the A-Z strip was set to both edges, and an always-on strip sat on top of the favorites.
