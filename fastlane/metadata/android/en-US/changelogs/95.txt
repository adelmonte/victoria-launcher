New:
* The clock widget can show what is coming up: the next calendar events and the next alarm, each off by default, each opening what it is about. Up to three events, as far ahead as you like, said as a clock time or as how long until.
* A custom date format for the clock widget, typed in yourself.
* Work profile apps say so after their name, so two copies of the same app can be told apart.
* The A-Z strip can leave the outermost edge of the screen to the system back gesture.

Reading your calendar asks permission the moment you switch it on, never at install, and nothing read can leave the phone: Victoria still has no network access.
